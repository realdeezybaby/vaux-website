Our process

A clear path from scattered marketing to growth.

Every engagement follows a defined sequence so the client always knows what is happening and why.

01

Discovery and fit

We learn how the business makes money, what the owner wants to achieve, who the ideal customer is, and whether Vaux is the right partner.

02

Business and brand audit

We review positioning, offers, website, content, advertising, tracking, lead handling, and current sales capacity.

03

Strategy and creative direction

We build the campaign angle, audience plan, content priorities, testing roadmap, and performance targets.

04

Production and build

Vaux coordinates the right specialists, directs the creative, prepares campaigns, and confirms tracking before launch.

05

Launch and activation

Campaigns launch in controlled stages so technical issues and weak creative can be corrected before scaling.

06

Optimization and reporting

We measure qualified leads, costs, conversion quality, creative performance, and recommended next actions.

07

Scale what works

Once the system proves itself, we expand budget, creative volume, offers, geography, or channels carefully.
