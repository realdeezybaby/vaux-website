The Vaux process

A growth engagement should never feel vague.

Every client moves through a defined sequence so strategy, production, launch, and optimization stay connected.

01

Qualification

Discovery and fit

We begin with a structured conversation about the business model, margins, customer value, sales process, current marketing, and capacity. The goal is not to force a package—it is to determine whether Vaux can create a measurable opportunity.

You leave with

  • A clearer definition of the current problem
  • A recommendation on the appropriate service level
  • Transparent next steps and expectations
02

Diagnosis

Business and brand audit

We inspect the offer, positioning, website, content, ads, tracking, lead handling, and customer journey. This stage identifies the bottleneck before money is spent trying to solve the wrong issue.

We review

  • Brand message and offer clarity
  • Creative quality and content consistency
  • Campaign history and tracking setup
  • Lead follow-up and sales conversion
03

Planning

Strategy and creative direction

Vaux creates the campaign angle, audience plan, content priorities, production needs, channel strategy, testing roadmap, and performance targets.

Deliverables may include

  • Campaign brief and messaging framework
  • Content shot list and creative references
  • Advertising funnel plan
  • Measurement and reporting plan
04

Execution

Production and build

Our specialist network handles the agreed creative work while Vaux directs the overall execution. Campaigns, edits, tracking, pages, and assets are prepared for launch and reviewed for consistency.

Quality controls

  • Creative approval before publishing
  • Tracking checks before launch
  • Mobile and desktop review
  • Clear ownership of every deliverable
05

Launch

Campaign activation

Campaigns go live in controlled stages. Early data is used to identify technical issues, weak creative, poor audience fit, or breakdowns in lead handling before budgets scale.

06

Improvement

Optimization and reporting

We review performance against business outcomes—not vanity metrics. Vaux tests creative, audience, message, and offer variables while documenting what is working and what needs to change.

Reporting focuses on

  • Qualified leads and booked opportunities
  • Cost per lead and conversion quality
  • Creative and campaign performance
  • Recommended next actions
07

Growth

Scaling what works

Once the system demonstrates reliable performance, we expand budget, creative volume, channels, geography, or offers in a controlled way. The goal is durable growth—not one lucky campaign.
