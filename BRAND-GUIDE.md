# VAUX Brand Guide

## Brand Position
VAUX Media Group is a premium, founder-led growth agency for ambitious local and service businesses.

## Visual Direction
Dark luxury. Cinematic, minimal, confident, and high-contrast. The reference feeling is Apple restraint, Nike confidence, and a high-end creative agency.

## Color Palette
- Obsidian: #070809
- Carbon: #0D1012
- Graphite: #171A1D
- Soft White: #F4F1EA
- Muted Silver: #A8A8A3
- VAUX Gold: #C89432
- Light Gold: #E0B45C

## Typography
- Display / headlines: Barlow Condensed, 700–800, uppercase
- Body / UI: Inter, 400–700
- Headlines should be short, assertive, and spacious.
- Gold is used selectively for emphasis, not large paragraphs.

## Photography
- Use real founder and client photography.
- Preserve sharpness and natural skin texture.
- Prefer dark, uncluttered or heavily softened backgrounds.
- Avoid collages, mirrored screenshots, baked-in navigation, fake UI, or mixed faces.

## Layout
- Wide spacing and strong hierarchy
- Large uppercase headlines
- Thin borders and subtle texture
- Minimal rounded corners
- One primary call to action per section

## Voice
Clear, confident, direct, strategic, and grounded in outcomes.
